Invoicing24 ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Invoicing24 online invoicing platform available at invoicing24.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

By creating an account or using Invoicing24, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through your use of the service.

Account information

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored in hashed form; we never store plain-text passwords)

Business and invoicing data

  • Company details (name, address, contact number, GST number, state)
  • Customer records (name, address, contact number, GST number, state)
  • Product and service items (name, description, HSN code, price, units)
  • Invoice data (invoice numbers, dates, line items, discounts, tax calculations, totals)

Technical information

  • Authentication tokens used to keep you signed in
  • Basic usage data such as browser type, device information, and access timestamps

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Invoicing24 account
  • Provide invoicing, customer management, item management, and PDF export features
  • Calculate GST and invoice totals based on the data you enter
  • Store and display your invoice history within your dashboard
  • Respond to support requests and service-related communications
  • Improve platform reliability, security, and user experience
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information or business data to third parties.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed before storage. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.

While we implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

4. Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: With trusted infrastructure or hosting partners who help us operate the platform, subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Business protection: To protect the rights, safety, and security of Invoicing24, our users, or the public

We do not share your invoice or customer data with other users of the platform.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information through your account settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@invoicing24.com.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Invoicing24 uses browser local storage and session mechanisms to maintain your login session and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can clear stored data through your browser settings, though this may sign you out of the application.

8. Children's Privacy

Invoicing24 is intended for business use and is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Users

Invoicing24 is designed for businesses operating in India, including GST-compliant invoicing. If you access the service from outside India, you understand that your data may be processed and stored in India or other locations where our service providers operate.
